# The Ocean Health Index is officially paused!

{{<newsHead>}}

We are proud of our 13 year run, but we are no longer updating the Ocean Health Index scores. 

Scores and methods from 2012 through 2025 will remain available on this site.

If you are interested in supporting the restart of the Ocean Health Index, please contact Ben Halpern at [halpern@nceas.ucsb.edu](mailto:halpern@nceas.ucsb.edu).
